A candidate proof is available that the marked-line construction underlying
OpenAI family 252 yields a torsion-free nonsofic word-hyperbolic group with a
finite two-dimensional classifying space. The new ingredient is a quantitative
rank obstruction stable under approximate permutation actions. The manuscript
also supplies a five-dimensional incidence construction, specified by a
terminating finite search rather than a materialized full presentation.
